Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes. The game works without a connection. The App only goes online to send scores to Game Center (rankings and achievements) and to open pages such as the Terms of Use from inside the app. Scores from play without a connection are sent the next time you sign in to Game Center.
How do I appear in the rankings?
Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings app. To see the rankings, open Records from the menu on the map screen, then tap Rankings.
How do I change the difficulty?
Open Settings from the menu on the map screen and choose Easy, Normal, or Hard. Records such as best scores are kept separately for each difficulty, while unlocked stages are shared across all difficulties.
Can I transfer my save data to a new iPhone?
The App has no transfer feature. If you move to a new iPhone using a backup of your old one, your save data may come along with it.
How do I delete my save data?
Deleting the App deletes all saved data. Deleted data cannot be restored.
Can I post gameplay videos or screenshots?
Yes, as long as you clearly credit the App by name (“Pirochan”). See Article 4 of the Terms of Use for details.
